Key Design Recommendations & Impact

  • Optimized External Shading: Crucial for Lusaka's high solar gains, reducing cooling loads by up to 25%.
  • Insulated Roof: High impact due to direct solar exposure. Recommended R-value of 2.5 m²K/W for roof insulation.
  • High Thermal Mass Walls: Utilize local materials like heavy masonry for diurnal temperature moderation.
  • Natural Ventilation Design: Incorporate operable windows and cross-ventilation strategies for comfort during mild periods.
  • Efficient Lighting & Daylighting: Maximise natural light to reduce artificial lighting load (35% of total energy).
