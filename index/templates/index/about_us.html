Our Mission

At Zambia Eco Design Toolkit (ZEDT), we're solving the energy crisis from the ground up. We believe that sustainable design shouldn't be a luxury—it should be the default. Our platform empowers designers to see the invisible: thermal bridges, solar gains, and carbon footprints.

By bringing industrial-grade simulation tools (like EnergyPlus) into an accessible web interface, we help you optimize buildings for regional climates before construction begins.

"The most sustainable building is the one that is designed to work with nature, rather than against it. Our mission is to make that harmony measurable and attainable for every architect."

— Zambia Eco Design Toolkit (ZEDT) Team
